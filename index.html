§01 THE IDEA

The world isn't stored as pixels. It's stored as relations.

I build robots that remember places the way brains might: a whole scene bound into one high-dimensional vector, queried by algebra instead of search. Neuromorphic sensing in, spiking hardware out — at ICNS, Western Sydney University.
