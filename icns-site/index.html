Featured · Perception

Seeing the lit and the eclipsed moon at once

A conventional sensor exposes for the bright limb and loses the shadowed disc entirely. Because every pixel in an event camera sets its own threshold and reports only change, both parts of the moon stay visible through the whole eclipse.

This is the plainest demonstration of the property that makes the technology useful everywhere else: a scene with extreme contrast, resolved without choosing which half to lose.
