Facility · operational since April 2024

DeepSouth

The world's first supercomputer built to simulate spiking neural networks at the scale of the human brain: 228 trillion synaptic operations per second on commodity FPGA hardware, at a fraction of the power of a conventional machine. Built at ICNS, open to collaborators.
