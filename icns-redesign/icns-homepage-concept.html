Space & defence·On orbit since 2022

Falcon Neuro

Our event cameras on the International Space Station, built with the United States Air Force Academy. They returned the first event-based images ever captured from orbit, watching lightning and upper-atmosphere sprites that conventional cameras cannot resolve.

First neuromorphic sensors used for Earth observation from orbit

Computation·Operating

DeepSouth

The first supercomputer built to simulate spiking neural networks at the scale of the human brain, on reconfigurable commodity hardware and at a fraction of the power a conventional machine would need. Built here, open to collaborators.

228 trillion synaptic operations per second

Agriculture & industry·In the field, New South Wales

Counting what moves

Event cameras separate a bee from moving vegetation by how it moves, not how bright it is. The same method finds anything too fast, too small or too repetitive to film: a pest in a greenhouse, a defect on a production line, a bearing starting to fail.

100–250 Hz wingbeat band, resolved without frames

One capability, four sectors. If your problem involves something moving too fast, too dim or too often for a conventional camera, it is probably one of these.

01

Space & defence

Tracking objects in daylight, from orbit, or through obscured air, where optical systems saturate or lose the target.

  • Falcon Neuro, cameras on the space station
  • Astrosite mobile observatory
  • Maritime situational awareness
  • Acoustic drone detection
Space & defence work
02

Agriculture & environment

Counting, classifying and tracking living things in cluttered outdoor scenes, on battery, with no network.

  • Honey bee waggle dance detection
  • Neuromorphic perception for greenhouses
  • Automated acoustic koala detection
  • Bushfire risk from acoustic scenes
Agriculture & environment work
03

Industry & manufacturing

Inspection and monitoring beyond frame rate: fast lines, vibration, anomalies that appear between frames. Capability established, seeking first industry partners.

  • Visual anomaly detection on aerial platforms
  • Spiking neural networks on chip
  • Neuromorphic processors at the edge

04

Health & assistive

Low-power sensing and auditory models applied to assessment, monitoring and hearing.

  • Automated child reading assessment
  • Speech progress monitoring for late talkers
  • Cochlear and auditory pathway models
