# ICNS copy deck

Text-only rewrites for the current site. Every change here is a text field in the publishing system, Squiz Matrix, editable by whoever holds the centre's subsite access. No design change, no template change, no approval beyond the usual.

Quoted "now" text was taken from the live pages on 24 August 2026. Where a field is generated by the system rather than typed, it is marked.

## What copy alone fixes, and what it does not

**Fixes:** the out-of-date DeepSouth story, the meta description Google shows, headings that read as 2010s portal labels, card summaries that cut off mid-word, buttons that do not say where they go, the two empty listing pages, the incomplete address, and the missing product names such as Falcon Neuro.

**Does not fix:** the rotating banner with no pause control, images with no dimensions or descriptions, the dead links, and the three overlapping menu sections. Those are template settings, link fields and structure. Worth knowing before anyone promises that a copy pass makes the site compliant, because it does not.

## Opportunities, vacancies and scholarships

Both listing pages show "No results were found", which the system generates and you cannot edit directly. You can add standing text above the listing, which is what these pages need.

**Current vacancies**, intro text: **Positions at the centre are advertised through the Western Sydney University careers site as they open, and usually include postdoctoral researchers, research engineers and technical staff. If nothing is listed below, we are still glad to hear from strong candidates. Write to ICNS@westernsydney.edu.au with a short note about what you work on.**

**Current scholarships**, intro text: **Doctoral candidates working on centre projects are normally supported by a university scholarship, applied for alongside the project. Start from the open doctoral projects and contact the named supervisor, rather than waiting for a scholarship to be listed here.**

## Research and applications pages

The three application pages are named Sensors, Algorithms and Processors. That is how the centre is built, not how a visitor searches. An agricultural business has a greenhouse, and a manufacturer has a line running faster than a camera can film. Neither types "algorithms".

If the page names can be changed, group by sector instead: **Space and defence**, **Agriculture and environment**, **Industry and manufacturing**, **Health and assistive**. If the names are fixed for now, add one sector paragraph to the top of each existing page so the words a partner searches for appear on the site at all.

Suggested opening for an industry and manufacturing section: **Event cameras report change rather than capturing frames, so they see what falls between frames: a defect on a fast line, a bearing starting to fail, a vibration too small and too quick to film. The centre has built this sensing for orbit and for bushland, and is looking for first manufacturing partners to apply it on a production line.**

That last sentence is deliberate. It states an aspiration as an aspiration, which is both honest and an invitation.

## Names to standardise

- **DeepSouth**, one word.
- **Falcon Neuro**, not Falcon Neuron. This is the name the Air Force Research Laboratory publishes it under, and the name a partner will search.
- **Master of Applied Neuromorphic Engineering**, the enrolled course name.
- Expand acronyms on first use on every page, keeping the acronym: "the International Space Station", "a non-disclosure agreement". Partners know them; the students and journalists reading the same page may not.
